# Budget tree & bookings — `backend/app/modules/budget`

**Does:** Hierarchical cost center budgeting. The tree carries fiscal years per top-level node, top-down allocations, bookings (expenses/income) with sub-bookings, transfers, and invoices imported from ZUGFeRD/Factur-X PDFs. The append-only audit log records every money mutation. Most mutations are revertable.

**Key files:**
- `access.py` — the read gate of the invoice and booking lists: `BUDGET_READ_PERMISSIONS` (`budget.view`/`.structure`/`.book`) and `has_budget_read(principal)`. The router (`_has_full_view`, `require_any_permission(*BUDGET_READ_PERMISSIONS)`), the global search and the to-do links use it, so no copy of the trio drifts.
- `tree_models.py` — SQLAlchemy models for the tree: `Budget`, `FiscalYear`, `BudgetAllocation`, `BudgetExpense`, `Invoice` (EUR-only, `Numeric(12,2)`).
- `models.py` — legacy flat-pot models `BudgetPot`/`BudgetField`/`BudgetEntry` (per-application 1:1 binding, `STAGES = requested→reserved→approved→paid`). The tree supersedes most of this. `BudgetEntry.stage` still drives the roll-up.
- `tree_rules.py` — pure, DB-free domain logic (100% branch-covered): path-key composition, descendant tests, fiscal year interval overlap, allocation invariants, `rollup_committed`, `node_available`, `build_forest`, `fiscal_year_bounds`, `scope_forest`.
- `tree/` — thin I/O wiring over `tree_rules`, one ops class per concern (`nodes`, `fiscal_years`, `allocations`, `applications`, `expenses`, `subbookings`, `invoices`, `transfers`, `revert`, `view`). `tree/service.py` combines them into `BudgetTreeService`.
- `plan_models.py`, `plan_rules.py` (pure), `plan_schemas.py`, `plan_router.py`, `tree/plans.py` (`PlanOps`) — the Haushaltsplan of a fiscal year (specs 000791-000855): `budget.plan_enabled`, `budget_plan` with its versions (`draft`, `in_progress`, `in_force`, `replaced`) and lines, and `budget_plan_step`, the ordered steps of a top-level budget (name, optional Gremium). A version that leaves the draft keeps the steps in `steps` (JSONB: `name`, `gremiumId`, `passedAt`, `voteId`) and passes them in order (`POST /budget-plan-versions/{id}/steps/next`, optional body `step`: the place of the step from 0, else 409 `plan_step_not_next`); a plan in force writes the allocations. `POST .../steps/next/request` asks the Gremium of the next step for its decision (`requestedAt` on the step, audit `budget_plan_step_request`, 422 `plan_step_no_gremium`, 409 `plan_step_waits`) and creates no vote: the vote runs in a meeting of that Gremium (spec 000838). `waiting_plans(gremium_id)` and `waiting_plan(version_id, gremium_id)` (404, 409 `plan_step_not_waiting`) are what `livevote` reads for the agenda; the agenda item holds `plan_version_id`, and its vote gets `vote.plan_version_id`. `VotingService.open` binds the vote to the step (`take_step_vote`, 409 `plan_step_vote_runs` while another vote of the step is open); `VotingService.close` calls `pass_step_by_vote` for an accepted vote and `refuse_step_by_vote` for every other result, which sends the version back to the draft with all steps open. Neither fails the close. `PlanVersionOut.meetings` lists the meetings that hold the version. The plan routes need `budget.structure`; a read follows the view of the top-level budget (`_require_node_view`). Each write in the tree of a budget first locks its top-level budget (`_lock_tree(budget_id)` in `tree/service_base.py`: one statement that finds the top level by the parent chain, `FOR NO KEY UPDATE OF budget`, and reads the row again), then reads its rows, then locks the plan row and reads the version again (`_version_context`, 404 when the plan or the version is gone). Every write of a plan, a cost center (create, update, key, delete, side), an allocation, a fiscal year and their reverts takes it, and the close of a step vote takes it before its audit entry (`VotingService._locked_plan`), so two requests never read around each other or stop each other (spec 000842). Read after the lock, never before, and never find the top level by the path key in a write, because a key can change. The fake sessions of the unit tests skip that statement (`is_plan_lock` in `tests/_support/auth_fakes.py`). A parent line always holds the sum of its children (`_resum`, also after a node delete and in a copy). Every plan starts as a draft (spec 000854). Switch on: a draft from the allocations per open year without a plan, and no allocation changes; a fiscal year created later gets a draft too, as a copy of the version in force of the latest earlier year, else from its allocations (`_plan_for_new_year`). Switch off only while each plan is one plain draft (`plan_rules.is_plain_draft`, else `plan_switch_has_plans`), and then 409 `plan_not_enabled` on each plan write. An allocation is set by hand until a version of the year is in force. `PUT /budget-plan-versions/{id}/state` (permission `budget.plan.set_state`, body `state`, `step`, `reason`) sets the state of the newest version by hand, in both directions, with the audit entry `budget_plan_state_set` (spec 000855); it has no MCP tool. `DELETE /budget-plan-versions/{id}` deletes a draft. Back to draft deletes the draft votes and cancels the open votes of the steps; the agenda items stay; a step with a vote is not passed by hand (`plan_step_has_vote`). The side belongs to the plan line of a Titel, a direct child of the budget (`budget_plan_line.side`, spec 000853; `plan_rules.side_of` gives a deeper line the side of its Titel). It is set only in a draft, through the line or the create in the version (422 `side_not_settable` for any other line), and the side income clears the Vermerke of the draft lines below (audit `clearedMarks`). A version leaves the draft only when every Titel has a side (422 `plan_side_missing`). `budget.plan_coverable_default` (top level, `planCoverableDefault` in the node update and the tree) makes a line start "deckungsfähig" at the moment it becomes an expense line: a new line below an expense Titel, and the lines of a Titel that gets the side expense (`plan_rules.starts_coverable`, audit `defaultedMarks`; spec 000874). A request that names `coverable` wins, and the setting changes no line that exists. Back to draft takes the lock of the votes first (`VotingService.lock_for_plan_version`), because the close of a vote locks the vote and then the plan. A fiscal year with a decided plan is not deleted and keeps its year (`fiscal_year_has_plan`), and not closed while a version is `in_progress` (`plan_in_progress`). No plan action is revertable. `plan_export.py` (pure) and `tree/plan_exports.py` (`PlanExportOps.plan_export`) build the export of a version (spec 000875): `GET /budget-plan-versions/{id}/export.xlsx` and `/export.pdf`, bare gate plus the read rule of the budget, audit `export`. One row model (`sheet_rows`) feeds the workbook (`shared/xlsx.build_table_workbook`, amounts as numbers) and the typst variant `plan` (`pdf_config`); the columns of the year before show when an earlier year has a plan in force, the columns of the version before only for a Nachtrag, and a version that is not in force carries its state as a mark; for a version in progress that is the name of its first open step. The PDF carries the logos of the corporate design that the Gremium of the budget holds now (`plan_design`, `files/cd_resolver.resolve_cd_variant_of_gremium`), else the default logo of the render service.
- `tree_router.py` — FastAPI router (`tags=["budget"]`), mounted in `main.py` under `/api`.
- `tree_schemas.py` — frozen Pydantic v2 camelCase DTOs (`FrozenCamelModel` from `app/shared/schemas.py`, `populate_by_name`).
- `invoice_import.py` — pure ZUGFeRD/Factur-X CII-XML extraction + mapping (`parse_zugferd_pdf`, `NotZugferdError`, `UnsupportedInvoiceCurrencyError`). The module imports `pycheval` and `pypdf` lazily.
- `stats.py` — `BudgetStatsService.refresh()` for materialized views `mv_budget_usage`, `mv_status_distribution`.

**Domain / data model:**
- `budget` — tree node. `parent_id` is a self-FK with `ON DELETE RESTRICT` (NULL = top-level). `key` = path segment (alphanumeric, ≤64, no `-`). `path_key` = composed `VS-800-04`, server-maintained and unique. The parent stays immutable. `PATCH` can change the `key`: `_rename_key` makes the `path_key` of the node and of its subtree again (409 `budget_key_exists`, spec 000246). `gremium_id` is set only at top-level. An application in a state with `state.is_approved` counts as bound (decision amount, else requested), one with `state.is_denied` with 0 €, every other as *requested* (specs 000571/000572; see `be-flow`). `fully_bound` = the whole subtree allocation counts as committed (available 0). `active` false hides the node in the Budget tab and in the pickers; the node still rolls up, and a person with `budget.structure` still sees it (spec 000834). `view_gremium_id` grants a scoped view of the subtree without global permissions. `decision_gremium_id` names the Gremium that decides on spending from the node and its subtree; the effective value is the own value, else the nearest ancestor's (`tree_rules.resolve_decision_gremium`, DB wrapper `budget/decision.py`). The API exposes `decisionGremiumId` (read/write, 422 `decision_gremium_invalid`; an unknown `viewGremiumId` gives 422 `view_gremium_invalid`), `effectiveDecisionGremiumId` and `decisionGremiumSourceId` (read). A vote state with `gremiumSource: "budget"` uses it (see `be-flow`). `fiscal_start_month`/`fiscal_start_day` (top-level only, day 1–28) define the start date of the fiscal year. Currency CHECK = EUR.
- `fiscal_year` — one per top-level budget, identified by `year`. `start_date`/`end_date` follow the start date of the top budget (`start = date(year)`, `end = date(year+1) − 1 day`). Unique `(budget_id, year)`. The intervals must not overlap inside one top-level budget. The display is `YYYY` for a start on 01.01., else `YYYY/YY`. `closed_at` (nullable) marks a closed year ("abgeschlossen"): every money mutation on it answers 409 `fiscal_year_closed` (bookings incl. sub-bookings, transfers, allocations, assign/move of an application into or out of it, audit revert, and the flow action `assignBudget` skips). The year of a closed fiscal year is fixed and a closed year cannot be deleted; the `active` flag stays free. A closed year never counts as the default year of `_resolve_fiscal_year`. The guard is `_require_open_fiscal_year` / `_refuse_closed` in `tree/service_base.py`. A flow state change of an assigned application can still move the bound/requested figures of a closed year.
- `budget_allocation` — top-down `(budget_id, fiscal_year_id, allocated)`. Invariant: Σ children allocated ≤ parent allocated, per fiscal year. **No roll-up** of allocation.
- `budget_expense` — actual booking. `kind = 'expense'` (expended, lowers the budget) or `'income'` (raises available). `application_id` (optional, NOT unique) binds a booking to an application and replaces its committed amount proportionally (`bound = max(0, amount − Σ bound expenses)`). The booking inherits the cost center and the fiscal year from the application. `invoice_id` and `transfer_id` link the invoice and the paired transfer row. `parent_expense_id` links a sub-booking to its parent, and the parent amount is the sum of its children. Metadata: `invoice_date`, `payment_date`, `correspondent`, `note`, `reference_number`, `payment_method` (ueberweisung|bar|lastschrift|karte|paypal), `category`. CHECKs: amount>0, EUR, valid kind and payment_method.
- `invoice` — standalone document, 1 invoice : N bookings (`SET NULL` on delete). Fields: number, issue/due dates, supplier, net/tax/gross, `status` open|paid, stored original PDF (`file_object_key`/name/mime in MinIO). EUR + status CHECKs.
- Roll-up rule: **allocation flows down, consumption flows up.** Allocation itself never rolls up. `committed = bound + expended`. `available = allocated − bound − expended + income`. The value may go negative, and that is intentional. Nothing clamps it.

**API surface (all principal-only, fail-closed, RBAC server-side):**
- `GET /api/budgets` — cost center forest with `pathKey` and per-fiscal-year allocated/bound/requested/expended/income/committed/available. Full view needs `budget.view`/`structure`/`book`, else the gremium scope of `view_gremium_id` applies.
- `POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/budgets[/{id}]` — node CRUD, `budget.structure`. Delete blocked (409) if children or allocations exist.
- `GET /api/budget/export.xlsx`, `GET /api/expenses/export.xlsx` — `budget.export`.
- `GET /api/budgets/{id}/applications` — applications in the node and its subtree, optional `fiscalYear`.
- `GET/POST /api/budgets/{id}/expenses`, `POST /api/expenses`, `GET /api/expenses` (paged, filtered and sorted), `PATCH/DELETE /api/budget-expenses/{id}` — bookings, `budget.book`. A list read allows any budget permission.
- `POST /api/budget-transfers` — cost center to cost center transfer (expense + income, same fiscal year), `budget.book`.
- `GET/POST/PATCH/DELETE /api/invoices`, `POST /api/invoices/parse` (ZUGFeRD), `POST /api/invoices/file`, `GET /api/invoices/{id}/file` — invoices. Write needs `budget.book`, read needs any budget permission. `InvoiceOut.linkedBookings` lists the bookings with that `invoice_id` (sub-bookings too, in booking order) as `InvoiceBookingOut` (`id`, `budgetId`, `pathKey`, `budgetName`, `fiscalYearId`, `kind`, `amount`, `description`, `paymentDate`, `parentExpenseId`, `createdAt`). One batch query per page. The list and the detail filter them by the budget visibility: the full view (`budget.view`/`.structure`/`.book`) sees all, otherwise `visible_budget_ids(member gremien)` (the set form of `can_view_node`: the subtrees of the nodes whose `view_gremium_id` is a member Gremium). Today the invoice read already needs a full-view permission, so the filter is defence in depth for a future gremium-scoped read. The list (`InvoicePage`) also takes `booked=true|false`: an invoice with or without a booking that the reader can see (the same visibility rule). With `status=open` this gives the list segments "Eingang" (open, no booking) and "Verbucht" (open, with a booking); "Bezahlt" is `status=paid`. `counts` (`all`, `inbox`, `booked`, `paid`) gives the size of each segment under every other filter of the request, from one aggregate query.
- `GET/POST/PATCH /api/budgets/{id}/fiscal-years[/{fyId}]`, `POST /api/budgets/{id}/fiscal-years/{fyId}/close|reopen` (audit `budget_fiscal_year_close`/`_reopen`), `PUT /api/budgets/{id}/allocations/{fyId}` — `budget.structure`.
- `POST /api/applications/{id}/assign-budget`, `POST /api/applications/{id}/move-fiscal-year` — `application.manage`.

**Conventions & gotchas:**
- CRITICAL module — `budget` requires 100% branch coverage. Keep every decision in `tree_rules.py` (pure) and keep the `tree/` ops classes (`tree/service.py`) as thin DB wiring. Test the rules in isolation.
- EUR only everywhere: DB CHECKs plus `_MAX_AMOUNT = 9999999999.99` `le` guards on input and on invoice import. A too-large amount must return 422, never a numeric-overflow 500.
- The fiscal start day is capped at 1–28 (schema + `is_valid_fiscal_start`) so the start date exists in every month. Otherwise `fiscal_year_bounds` raises `ValueError`, which becomes a 422.
- Allocation invariants raise 422 (`children_allocation_exceeds_parent`, `parent_allocation_below_children`). A fiscal year overlap gives 422. A delete with children or allocations gives 409. All errors use RFC-9457 problem+json.
- The audit log records every money mutation (`AuditAction.BUDGET_*`, see `[[be-audit]]`). `revert_audit` dispatches the revertable actions (`REVERTABLE_BUDGET_ACTIONS`). It deletes an additive operation such as an expense, a transfer or a node create. It restores an update from the audit `data` snapshot. `stale_revert` and `already_reverted` give 409. Deletes are NOT revertable, and that is deliberate (see `[[revert-feature-scope]]`).
- A booking with an `invoice_id` does NOT change the status of that invoice. The service only checks that the invoice exists (404). The invoice stays `open` ("Verbucht" in the list, also for a part booking) until a user sets `status: paid` with `PATCH /api/invoices/{id}` ("Als bezahlt markieren"). A revert of the booking keeps the invoice status. Older audit entries can show `BUDGET_INVOICE_UPDATE` with reason `expense_booked`: that is the former auto-pay.
- Invoice import: `pycheval` is a strict EN16931 validator and fails on real-world PDFs. The module extracts the CII-XML with `pypdf` instead and falls back to the tolerant `_parse_cii_header`. It avoids `dict(reader.attachments)`, because that call decompresses ALL streams and opens a DoS. `extract_facturx_from_pdf` from `pycheval` loops forever on an attachment name other than `factur-x.xml`. The embedded XML is capped at 16 MiB.
- Invoice file serving forces `application/pdf` and `Content-Disposition: attachment`. It ignores a client-supplied `file_mime`, so no PDF polyglot renders in the app origin. The server must issue every file token under the `invoices/` prefix (`_validate_invoice_file_token`). Uploads get a size cap (`body_cap`), a MIME check and a virus scan.
- Never show a raw UUID in the UI. The server resolves `ExpenseOut.actorName` and `applicationTitle` (see `[[no-uuids-in-ui]]`).
- An `income` booking can never link to an application (`ExpenseCreate` validator).
