# The start page explains the app in Leichte Sprache and DGS

The public start page must link to an explanation in Leichte Sprache and to a video in
Deutsche Gebärdensprache. Both must explain the content of the app, the navigation and the
accessibility statement.

Reason: § 10 L-BGG with § 4 BITV 2.0 requires these two explanations on the start page.
