# Every view meets WCAG 2.2 level AA

Every view of the app must meet the success criteria of WCAG 2.2 at level A and level AA.
This includes the public pages, the installed app and the beamer view. The zoom block of the
installed app (NF/compatibility/pwa/000220) is an exception. The accessibility statement must
name it.

Reason: The Studierendenschaft is a public body, so § 10 L-BGG applies. The law refers to
EN 301 549, and EN 301 549 V4.1.1 refers to WCAG 2.2 AA. WCAG 2.2 AA also covers every
criterion of WCAG 2.1 AA.

Test: The rules in `NF/accessibility/` and a review against the WCAG 2.2 AA list.
