# The Haushaltsplan has a page in the Budget tab

The Haushaltsplan must have the page `/budget/plan`. The ⋮ menu in the head of the Budget
tab must have the entry "Haushaltsplan" that opens the page.

Reason: A person who reads the budget must find the plan in the same tab.
