# The steps of a budget are edited at its Kostenstelle

The edit form of a top-level Kostenstelle must show the switch "Haushaltsplan" (000814)
and, while the switch is on, the list of plan steps. A person must add a step, name it,
choose its Gremium or none, move it and remove it.

Reason: The person who sets up a budget sets up the path of its plan in the same place.
