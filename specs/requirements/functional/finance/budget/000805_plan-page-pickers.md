# The plan page chooses Kostenstelle, year and version with chips

The plan page must have three filter chips: the top-level Kostenstelle, the fiscal year and
the version. The version chip must name the Stammplan and each Nachtrag.

Reason: One plan belongs to one top-level Kostenstelle, one fiscal year and one version.
