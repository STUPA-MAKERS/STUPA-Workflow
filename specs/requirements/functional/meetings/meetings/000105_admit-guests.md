# The meeting lead admits each guest

A guest scans the QR code and enters a name. The meeting lead admits or rejects the request.
A rejected guest can ask again after 3 minutes. The join page offers "Mitglied? Mit Konto
anmelden". A member who logs in there goes to the meeting.
