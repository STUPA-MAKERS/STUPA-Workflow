# The meeting lead admits each guest

A guest scans the QR code and enters a name. The meeting lead must admit or reject the request.
A rejected guest must be able to ask again after 3 minutes. The join page must offer "Mitglied?
Mit Konto anmelden". A member who logs in there must go to the meeting.
